Close Quarters

EDUCATION, a concept album about going through school at NYU, is out now.

WithNick Benak AlbumEDUCATION — psychedelic rock concept album · out 13 November 2026 ListenAll services · Spotify · Apple Music

A band with Nick Benak, written and recorded together. EDUCATION is a concept album about going through school at NYU: relationships, ambition, what success is supposed to look like, and how little of it resolves while you are still in it.

The band’s earlier releases are on Spotify, including “Whispers,” “Mood Change,” “Gilded Girl,” “Magical Land,” “Take it Out,” “Serenade,” “Hypnotized” and “Close Quarters Christmas.” EDUCATION is out now on every streaming service, and it streams here in full.
