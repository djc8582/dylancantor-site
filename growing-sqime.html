Growing

One collaboration, a cast of characters.

WithMiller Fishback StylesRap, jazz-rock, electronic RoleGuitar, modular synthesizer, production, engineering and mixing

A cast of characters made with Miller Fishback, split across two groups that started out separately and grew into one another.

Whippet is an avant-garde, experimental jazz-rock fusion jam band from Philadelphia, founded by Lord Whippet in September 2022 with a rotating circle of friends and percussionists. Fritz Rango joined later, and today he and Bruner lead the band, with Lord Whippet and iLLER BOYS among its members. Its catalogue runs from podcast music and city sounds to space odysseys and legendary tales: The Tale of a Warrior Named Whippet, The Space Wizard’s Apprentice, a Christmas special, a live record, and a long run of “…and the Whippets” records.

Ozstriker, Finch and Golden Joe have worked together as a trio since Interdimensional Cable and Bird Feeder. Bruner is a spirit of Ozstriker, and that thread is how the two groups meet: Fritz Rango and Bruner led Whippet into working with the trio, and Fritz and Finch now share credits on most of the catalogue, on tracks like “Superman,” “Lord of the Flies” and “hear the birds.”
