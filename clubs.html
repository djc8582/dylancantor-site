In the last thirty days the app handled 24,172 swipes, 3,530 applications and 2,133 messages. Signups went from 71 in April to 674 in October.

Matching people without a form

Onboarding asks you to describe yourself in your own words, in free text, and a pipeline turns that into structured interests, skills and goals — with moderation in the same pass. Project creation generates tags the same way. Search is semantic rather than keyword. There is also an email path: forward a message describing a project and it comes out the other side as a published card.

The discovery feed runs on a sawtooth-staircase ranking algorithm, batched and preloaded in the background so the first card paints instantly rather than after a spinner.

The toll-fraud attack

Someone discovered the SMS verification endpoint and began driving traffic through it to premium-rate numbers. Every fraudulent request cost real money.

I hardened the one-time-passcode flow against abuse — rate limiting, the obvious geographic and numbering-plan checks, and a set of heuristics on request shape. It is the kind of problem you do not design for in advance and then have to fix at speed, in production, while real users are signing up.
