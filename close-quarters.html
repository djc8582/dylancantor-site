Close Quarters

A concept album about going through school at NYU.

WithNick Benak AlbumEDUCATION — psychedelic rock, one continuous 49:17 piece ListenSpotify

A band with Nick Benak, written and recorded together. EDUCATION is a concept album about going through school at NYU: relationships, ambition, what success is supposed to look like, and how little of it resolves while you are still in it.

The band’s earlier releases are on Spotify, including “Whispers,” “Mood Change,” “Gilded Girl,” “Magical Land,” “Take it Out,” “Serenade,” “Hypnotized” and “Close Quarters Christmas.” EDUCATION streams here in full.
