Growing Sqime

One collaboration, a cast of characters.

WithMiller Fishback StylesRap, jazz-rock, electronic RoleGuitar, modular synthesizer, production, engineering and mixing

A collective of characters made with Miller Fishback. Every alias has its own register, so the project moves between rap, jazz-rock and electronic music without settling on one voice. Almost none of it is made alone.

Finch and Fritz Rango are the center of it: they share credits on most of the catalogue, and those records bring in outside voices, including Open Mike Eagle on “for you (it’s so good)” and Quelle Chris on “solstice.” Ozstriker works mostly with Golden Joe and 6teen, and joins Finch and Fritz on tracks like “Superman” and “Lord of the Flies.” Bruner and Whippet release mostly solo work, and meet on “it’s too hard to say goodbye.” Lord Whippet and iLLER BOYS link up with Fritz Rango on “DARKNESS RISING (WAY UP).” The most-played track across the aliases is Finch’s “empty pockets” with dkj, at over 650,000 Spotify plays (November 2026).
